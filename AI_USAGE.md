# AI use statement

This statement covers the research materials and manuscript preparation for “Autorschaft unter Begründungsdruck. Generative KI in chinesischen Literaturdebatten, deutschen Literaturportalen und auf BookTok”, together with the accompanying repository, version 1.0.

## Material exploration, coding and translation

AI assistance was used for initial paraphrases and codes, thematic grouping and selected assignments. The retained preliminary coding scripts configure `claude-sonnet-4-5` through the Anthropic API; this configuration does not establish the exact model snapshot served during the historical runs. German DeepL translations were used in the initial exploration of Chinese texts. The documented scope, input limits and checks are described in [Data collection and verification](docs/Erhebung_und_Pruefung.md).

## Authorship

Christoph auf der Horst wrote the original manuscript in summer 2026 and subsequently revised it, drawing on discussions in Düsseldorf and Changchun. The content, argument, structure and final manuscript are the author’s work and responsibility.

## Language assistance

ChatGPT/Codex (OpenAI) assisted with the iterative linguistic revision of the manuscript.  The author guided the work by providing feedback and corrections, as well as by selecting and revising the material included in the manuscript.

## Code, figures and repository documentation

ChatGPT/Codex supported the development and revision of calculation, validation and figure-generation scripts, as well as preparation and English translation of repository documentation. The manuscript figures are generated from the documented data with the included scripts. The technical checks and their limits are recorded in [the validation report](VALIDIERUNG.md).

## Verification, version records and responsibility

The [case-index guide](docs/Belegstellen_v12.md) and [methodological documentation](docs/Erhebung_und_Pruefung.md) distinguish archived-text checks, online source checks, translation checks and the limits of the available material. Computational tests do not establish independent coding or linguistic verification of the whole corpus.

Exact historical model versions are not consistently documented for ChatGPT/Codex or all exploratory steps. The DeepL version is not separately recorded. Available configuration evidence is identified as such rather than treated as a verified historical model snapshot.

The author is responsible for the selection and assessment of incorporated material, the interpretation of the evidence, and the final manuscript and published research materials. AI tools are acknowledged as tools and are not listed as authors.

# Materials and selection

The exploratory collections were used to locate relevant configurations of discourse. The article’s close readings draw on a purposive selection from these collections. The three material areas differ in collection procedures, publication formats and temporal concentration; their case counts are not comparable measures of national attitudes.

## German literary portals

The inventory contains 232 entries and the historical coding table 203. The two collections overlap at 190 URLs; thirteen coded entries supplement the inventory. Their union therefore comprises 245 entries. Of the 203 coded entries, 55 were classified as having a strong or medium connection to AI. Excluding one duplicate and one archive overview leaves 53 documents. Of these, 49 belong to the two portals used for the word-form analysis: thirteen from literaturkritik.de and 36 from literaturcafe.de. Four other sources are outside this analysis.

The close readings use seven main articles: DE-018, DE-025, DE-027, DE-158, DE-162, DE-164 and DE-176. Two additional comments (DE-164-K1 and DE-164-K2) respond to the same production report. They provide different objections to that project, rather than a representative distribution of audience opinions.

## Chinese literary discourse

The inventory of contributions published since January 2023 contains 962 entries from China Writer’s AI section and four literature sections of wenyi.gmw.cn. A sample allocated by source quotas and distributed over time comprises 120 texts of at least 800 Chinese characters. Twenty-six contributions with a strong AI connection and six with a medium connection form a core of 32 texts.

The six main contributions used in close reading are CN-007, CN-018, CN-019, CN-021, CN-022 and CN-023. All appeared on China Writer between June 2024 and March 2025. Five were published in February or March 2025, in the context of the DeepSeek debate. This concentration limits the comparison: the Guangming material is not separately examined through close readings.

The additional journalistic report CN-ERG-SHI-2025 clarifies the reporting of Chinese announcements excluding AI submissions. It is not one of the six main Chinese contributions. The analysis concerns the journalistic account, rather than the enforcement of an editorial rule.

## BookTok

API retrieval produced 22,563 entries by combining literary hashtags with AI search terms and by searching in the reverse direction. Screening covered 140 German posts in three rounds (40, 30 and 70) and 80 English posts. This yielded 23 German A-cases from nineteen accounts and eleven English A-cases from eleven accounts. Six German B-cases provide additional contrast material.

The current analysis uses six German A-cases (BT-DE-A-005, -007, -009, -021, -022 and -023) and two English A-cases (BT-EN-A-001 and -007). It examines saved captions and, for two German cases, saved speech transcripts. It does not cover the complete audiovisual posts or platform communication as a whole.

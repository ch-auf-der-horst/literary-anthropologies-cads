# Case index for manuscript v12

[belegstellen_v12.csv](../data/belegstellen_v12.csv) records the evidence used: 21 main contributions, two additional comments and one additional journalistic report. Section references correspond to manuscript v12. The case selection and source locations were checked against the accepted v12 text and its final editing.

The columns record case ID, evidence type, section, material, speaker role, year, source location, evidential function, scope, public source URL and verification status. The descriptions retain their German wording. An [English field guide](../data/README.md) explains the column names and evidence types. Empty source URLs for platform posts are intentional; private identification keys are not published.

## Source checks

The German portal evidence was checked against archived article bodies. For the Chinese evidence, the original passages, revised German working translations and speaker contexts were consulted. The four central translations remained unchanged during final editing. Platform evidence was checked against saved captions and, for two cases, saved speech transcripts.

The two portal comments were checked against the production report’s public comment section on 18 November 2026. They represent different objections to the same project and do not support claims about the distribution of audience opinions. BT-DE-A-021’s claim that AI reviews can be recognised was checked again against its archived caption and made explicit in the manuscript.

Shi Chenlu’s additional report was checked against the portal on 18 November 2026. It distinguishes the reported exclusion announcements by Shikan and Qingchun Shikan and records a later clarification. The analysis concerns the journalistic account, not the enforcement of an editorial rule.

The scope of linguistic and methodological checks is described in [Data collection and verification](Erhebung_und_Pruefung.md).

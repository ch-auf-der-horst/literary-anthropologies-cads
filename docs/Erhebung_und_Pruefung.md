# Data collection and verification

## Preliminary coding and category formation

The retained preliminary coding scripts configure `claude-sonnet-4-5` through the Anthropic API. According to the project files, this preliminary coding covers 177 of 203 German entries and all 120 Chinese entries. Inputs comprised at most the first 8,000 characters plus metadata; German DeepL translations were used for Chinese texts. The script configuration does not establish which historical model snapshot was actually served. The DeepL version is not separately documented either.

Model assistance is also documented for the Chinese thematic family grouping, the assignment of 29 additional German entries and the German BookTok clustering. Model versions for these exploratory steps are not consistently documented. The published family counts recalculate the retained assignments; they do not rerun those procedures.

## Text checks and translations

The author’s retrospective documentation describes a mixed procedure combining model suggestions with intensive checking, predominantly against full articles, including comparison of paraphrases, codes and short quotations. German texts were checked in the original and Chinese texts through German translations. This reading of full articles does not retrospectively expand the limited inputs to the earlier coding process.

Original passages, revised German working translations and speaker contexts were assembled for the Chinese evidence used in the article. According to the author, a Chinese-speaking reader compared four central translated quotations with the original passages. This does not amount to linguistic verification of the entire Chinese corpus. Source and speaker attribution are documented in the [case index](../data/belegstellen_v12.csv).

The BookTok analysis uses saved post texts and, for two German cases, speech transcripts. Checking these textual layers differs from viewing the complete videos. The two additional portal comments and the additional Chinese journalistic report were checked against public sources on 18 November 2026; details appear in the [case-index guide](Belegstellen_v12.md).

## Scope of verification

Automatically populated confirmation fields are not treated as evidence of human reading. There is no independent multiple coding with an established intercoder reliability measure. The [technical checks](../VALIDIERUNG.md) cover calculations, flow conservation, figure files and file integrity. Interpretations of individual cases must be assessed against their source contexts.

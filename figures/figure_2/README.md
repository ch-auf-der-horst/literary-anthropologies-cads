# Justifications of human authorship

Publication figure, 120 × 160 mm, accompanying manuscript v12, section 8. The interpretative synthesis connects experience and a work’s personal history, work on the text, and public attribution. The central fork asks about the creative status of human participation. Case IDs refer to the case index. Connectors indicate analytical relationships rather than frequencies.

The graphic and its German reading text retain the language used in the manuscript. An [English description](Description.en.md) explains its content and labels.

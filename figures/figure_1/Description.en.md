# Figure 1 · Selection pathways and close readings

The three lanes depict different selection procedures. Flow widths are scaled to the starting collection within each lane and cannot be read as common counts across lanes. The right-hand fields show the purposive selection of 21 main contributions. Their thin connectors are not proportional to case counts.

In the German material, the inventory and coding table overlap at 190 URLs. The 203 coded entries include 55 with a strong or medium AI connection; after two exclusions, 53 documents remain. Of these, 49 belong to the two portals used for word-form analysis. Seven articles are selected for close reading. Two comments on DE-164 supplement the analysis of responses to the same experiment.

For the Chinese material, selection proceeds from an inventory of 962 entries through a sample of 120 texts, allocated by source quotas and distributed over time, to a core of 32 AI-related contributions. All six close-reading cases are from China Writer. The Guangming material is therefore not independently examined through close readings.

For BookTok, 220 screened posts are shown from a retrieval collection of 22,563 entries. Screening 140 German posts yielded 23 A-cases; screening 80 English posts yielded eleven A-cases. Six German B-cases form contrast material. The current argument uses six German and two English A-cases.

## Reading the German labels

`Inventar` means inventory; `Kodiertabelle` means coding table; `stark/mittel` denotes a strong or medium AI connection; `Wortzählung` means word-form analysis; `Stichprobe` means sample; `KI-Kern` means AI-related core; and `Nahlektüren` means close readings. `Ausschluss` marks excluded material, `extern` marks sources outside the two German portals, and `B-Kontrast` marks BookTok contrast material.

Selection parameters are documented in [Materials and selection](../../docs/Material_und_Auswahl.md); the [case index](../../data/belegstellen_v12.csv) identifies the evidence used. The figure documents the recorded collection and its interpretative use, rather than a new data collection.
